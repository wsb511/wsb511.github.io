WSB Utilities provides software tools that may connect to supported third-party services when a user explicitly authorizes access.

Data Access

The software may access data from connected services only to perform functions initiated by the user and only within the permissions granted by the user.

Use of Data

Accessed data is used solely to provide requested functionality, including data organization, synchronization, processing, and workflow automation.

Data Storage

Where storage is required, data may be stored in services or systems controlled by the user or by the service provider selected by the user. Data retention is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the requested functionality.

Data Sharing

User data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. Data is not shared with unrelated third parties except when necessary to provide a user-requested function, comply with law, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

Authorization and Revocation

Users control access through the authorization mechanisms provided by each connected service and may revoke previously granted access at any time through the relevant service account settings.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of data.

Changes

This policy may be updated when functionality or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
